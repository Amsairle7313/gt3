Four litres.
No turbo.
Nine thousand rpm.

The 911 GT3 RS, 992 generation.

Scroll

Aerodynamics first.

A swan-neck wing, active front flaps and a drag-reducing flap in the wing. Together they press the car down with 409 kg at 200 km/h, and 860 kg at 285.

Brakes from the track.

Six-piston fixed calipers and 408 mm discs on the front axle, behind wheels that carry the weight and none of the excess.

Light where it counts.

Carbon-fibre hood and fenders, and a kerb weight of 1,450 kg. Take the panels away and what remains is a chassis, an engine and the aero that serves them.

Built to rev.

A naturally aspirated 4.0-litre flat-six. 525 PS at 8,500 rpm, 465 Nm, and a limit of 9,000 rpm, through a seven-speed PDK.
